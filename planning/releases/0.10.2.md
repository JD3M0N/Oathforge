# Oathforge 0.10.2 — anuncios

Interfaz (F01, 3.ª parte): inventario ordenado e iconos. Detalle en [`../../CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).

## English announcement — draft, not sent

🛡 Oathforge v0.10.2: A tidy backpack

🎒 Inventory with tabs
• The summary shows what you're wearing and how many items of each kind you carry.
• Tabs for ⚔️ Gear, 🧪 Consumables, 🧱 Materials and 🧰 Tools: one line per item, no walls of text.
• Equip from Gear, drink from Consumables, and open any /item_ page to equip or use it right there.

🪨 An icon for everything
• Ores 🪨, logs 🪵, herbs 🌿, hides 🐗, meat 🥩, fish 🐟, tools ⛏️… always next to the name.
• You'll see them in your loot, recipes, shops and in combat.

That wraps up the first big interface pass of 0.10. Tell us what still feels clunky! See you in Crownreach!
